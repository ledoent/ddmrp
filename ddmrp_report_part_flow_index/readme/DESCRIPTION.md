Classifies DDMRP stock buffers by their order frequency ("flow"), into
user-defined Flow Index Groups.

Two complementary views are provided:

- **Plan Flow Index** -- the *planned* order frequency derived from the
  buffer sizing (Green Zone / ADU).
- **Actual Flow Index** -- the *observed* order frequency derived from the
  real completed replenishments over the past horizon.

Comparing planned vs actual flow highlights buffers whose real ordering
behaviour diverges from their sizing.
